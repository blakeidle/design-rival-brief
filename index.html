The Design AI Bundling Shift: Canva vs. Figma vs. Adobe Express

The point: All three platforms now sell AI as a metered entitlement on seats, not a free side feature. Canva upsells AI Pass on Pro/Business. Figma attaches hard monthly AI credits to Full/Dev/Collab seats. Adobe Express keeps a low Premium sticker and steers heavy generation into Firefly Pro.

Scoreboard (US list prices)

RivalEntry paidMid / teamAI model
Canva Pro US$144/yr Business US$250/yr per person (Recommended); Enterprise “Let’s talk” Plan AI access + AI Pass monthly add-on
Figma Professional Full $16/mo (+ 3,000 AI credits) Org Full $55/mo (+ 3,500); Ent Full $90/mo (+ 4,250) Seat credits; purchasable top-ups
Adobe Express Premium ~$9.99/mo (often with 250 Firefly credits) Teams/Enterprise business plans; Firefly Pro ~$19.99/mo (~4,000 credits) Low sticker + Firefly credit ladder
Canva Pro/Business list prices match the Oct 4, 2026 Competitor Watch baseline screenshots. The motion in this window is AI upsell (AI Pass banner on pricing), not a headline Pro/Business price change.

Three packaging philosophies

Canva sells an annual design seat (5 Brand Kits on Pro → 100 on Business → 1000 on Enterprise) and meters Premium/Ultra AI; AI Pass markets up to ~40× Pro / ~20× Business allowance.

Figma makes seats the product. Full seats carry 3,000–4,250 monthly AI credits; Dev/Collab get 500. Credits reset monthly, do not roll over, and are not transferable. Agentic Make / agent tools consume credits (GA noted Oct 6, 2026).

Adobe Express stays cheap as a template/brand layer; Express Premium is also included in many Creative Cloud plans (Adobe Help, Jul 13, 2026). Heavy Firefly volume lives on a separate Firefly ladder.

Counter-moves

  1. Build an AI unit-economics sheet: seat + expected Pass/credits for your real weekly workflow.
  2. Watch Brand Kit ceilings — Canva Pro’s 5 kits remain an agency upgrade trigger.
  3. Expect AI Pass / credit packs to become silent ARR while list prices look flat.
